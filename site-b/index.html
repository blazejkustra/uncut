Say the cut.
Watch it happen.

Uncut is a video editor for the Mac with an agent built in. It runs your installed Claude Code, which edits a real timeline while you watch and hands every change back to you to keep or undo.

Download for Mac Source on GitHub

For Apple silicon Macs. Free and open source.

Ask the way you would ask an editor

The agent sees your timeline, your footage and its transcript. Each request becomes one turn, and every turn goes the same way.

Every turn is one undo. Keep what works, undo the rest, or carry on by hand on the same timeline.

Everything it does, you can do by hand

Tracks, trims, splits, snapping, keyframes and a mixer. A real editor underneath the agent, on the same timeline.

Edit by transcript
Transcription runs on your Mac. Remove fillers and long pauses in one click, or cut by the words.
Captions and titles
Burned in or word by word, SRT and WebVTT in and out, and animated titles, lower thirds and end cards.
Sound and music
A built-in sound library, online sound search, and a mixer that ducks the music under speech.
Capture
Record your screen, camera and mic, or iOS simulators, Android emulators and apps, straight into the project.
Effects and looks
Filters, colour looks, transitions and text animations, previewed on the GPU.
Plugins
Looks, components, checks and agent tools in a folder, with a catalog to browse.
Export
H.264 at the quality you pick, with loudness for social or broadcast and optional subtitles.

Your footage stays on your Mac

Uncut runs on your Mac, and so does transcription. The agent is your own Claude Code, with whatever sign-in it already uses, billed to you as your Claude Code usage normally is. Usage data is off unless you turn it on.
